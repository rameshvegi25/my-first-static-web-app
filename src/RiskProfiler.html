Before You Begin
A quick note before we begin

You may notice some questions feel similar to each other. That's entirely intentional.

This questionnaire uses a technique called psychometric cross-validation — the same methodology used by professional financial institutions worldwide. Asking the same idea in different ways, and in different emotional contexts, helps surface your true relationship with risk — not just your first instinct.

Your first answer to a question and your answer when the same idea is reframed often differ. That gap is precisely what reveals how you'd actually behave when markets move — and that's what matters most for building the right portfolio for you.

There are no right or wrong answers. Only honest ones.
